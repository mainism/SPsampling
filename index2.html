Uploaded GIS: 54 numbered DNCC polygons, 75 numbered DSCC polygons and four named non-ward areas. New GeoJSON and Shapefile have identical shapes and records; the CSV matches their attributes.

Real: Latest supplied KML alignment, stations, cell polygons, BBS/City Population 2022 ward population and thana totals. Each cell’s area share is the exact intersection of full-resolution GIS polygons in EPSG:32646.

Income: Fictional DEMO low/middle/high percentages until the team uploads a BBS-based income CSV. Never interpret these as measured ward income.

Boundary quality: All 133 shapes valid, no overlapping interiors, 54 DNCC and 75 DSCC numbered wards. Validate revision date and legal ward extents with DNCC/DSCC before statutory or final field allocation.

Area share ≠ household share. Population reported for a ward is the whole ward, not the portion intersecting a survey cell. Cantonments and Rayerbazar Boddho Bhumi are separately mapped and not assigned ward population.

GPS: Select a survey cell → Set as my target → allow GPS → enable browser notifications. The chosen cell turns green when inside and red when outside. Outside notifications repeat every 10 minutes only while the page is running and receiving fresh GPS updates. Mobile browsers may suspend background GPS/timers; notifications are not guaranteed if this tab is closed, the phone is locked, or battery saving stops the browser.
